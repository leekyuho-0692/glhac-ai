Core Features

An AI workflow that understands certification practice Built for real halal certification

🤖

AI Pre-Assessment

AI pre-screens application details and raw materials to flag risky ingredients, assess low-risk eligibility, and recommend a certification path.

🛤️

Dual-Path Determination

Based on BPJPH criteria (revenue, number of factories/outlets, low-risk materials), it automatically branches between Self-Declare and the Regular path.

📄

Document AI Classification · OCR

Automatically classifies uploaded documents — business licenses, process flow diagrams, SJPH manuals, halal certificates — and extracts key fields.

🔬

SJPH · HPAS Evaluation

Automatically tallies the 5 elements of the Halal Product Assurance System and HPAS items, merging them with auditor determinations to track audit completeness.

⚖️

Fatwa Committee

Records ketetapan halal decisions with supporting basis via member signatures, quorum, and self-declaration committee verification.

🎫

Digital Certificate

Manages the full post-issuance lifecycle in one place — QR public verification, electronic signature, scope freeze, and renewal derivation.

Role-based Collaboration

One platform, six perspectives Role-based collaboration

🏢Applicant Company

Create cases, register products and raw materials, upload documents, and enter self-declaration requirements.

🧭Consultant

Review applications, request supplementation, make document-level determinations, and provide consulting support.

🔍Auditor

Conduct mock and on-site audits, raise non-conformity findings, review corrective actions (CAR), and make HPAS determinations.

🕌Sharia Committee

Deliberate in the Fatwa committee, verify self-declaration committees, and issue ketetapan halal decisions.

⚙️Operator

Approve companies, assign auditors, manage payments, and request certificate issuance.

🛡️Admin

Oversee everything — full monitoring, audit logs, menu and permission assignment, and system settings.

Trust & Governance

The accountability certification bodies need Accountability by design

In halal certification, trust is everything. Hard-to-reverse decisions come with dual control, evidence, and audit trails by default.

✍️

Two-Person Approval

Certificate issuance and revocation separate requester from approver (maker-checker).

🚦

Evidence-based Guards

Required documents, payment, corrections, and committee decisions must be met before the next stage.

🔗

Audit Trail

State transitions are linked with an HMAC hash chain to prevent tampering and forgery.

🔐

Role & Permission Control

RBAC, organization isolation, and token revocation limit access to least privilege.
